A Beach Weekend Devotional

Anchored

Four days for the two of us. To reconnect, get rooted, and finally rest.

"Which hope we have as an anchor of the soul, both sure and stedfast." Hebrews 6:19

Ethan & Caroline · Thursday–Sunday

Thu · Day 1 Fri · Day 2 Sat · Day 3 Sun · Day 4

Each day builds on the last, the way a chain runs down to what actually holds. Read it together, slow down where you want to, and let the questions turn into conversation.

Day One · Thursday

Becoming Our Own

Our marriage was never just about leaving family behind. It's about becoming a new anchor point, one that whatever family comes after us will trace back to. Every home starts somewhere. This weekend, ours starts by choosing what we carry forward and what we leave on the shore.

Prayer

Ask God to root your marriage first in him. Pray that as a couple you would become a steady anchor for each other, rather than drifting toward whatever shaped you growing up.

Genesis 2:24

Therefore shall a man leave his father and his mother, and shall cleave unto his wife: and they shall be one flesh.

Hebrews 6:19

Which hope we have as an anchor of the soul, both sure and stedfast, and which entereth into that within the veil.

Talk it through
  • Looking back at our family motto, how have we actually lived that out recently? Where have we drifted from it?
  • What's one pattern from our parents' marriages we want to intentionally do differently?

Day Two · Friday

Gripping Solid Ground

An anchor only holds if it's actually gripping something solid. The question isn't whether we believe the Bible. It's whether our confidence today is gripping God's word, or just gripping how we feel.

Prayer

Ask God for a real hunger for his word this weekend, not obligation. Pray that his truth would feel like solid ground under both of you.

Psalm 1:1-3

Blessed is the man that walketh not in the counsel of the ungodly, nor standeth in the way of sinners, nor sitteth in the seat of the scornful. But his delight is in the law of the LORD, and in his law doth he meditate day and night. And he shall be like a tree planted by the rivers of water, that bringeth forth his fruit in his season; his leaf also shall not wither, and whatsoever he doeth shall prosper.

Colossians 2:7

Rooted and built up in him, and stablished in the faith, as ye have been taught, abounding therein with thanksgiving.

Talk it through
  • What's one thing God has shown us recently through his word that actually changed how we thought or acted?
  • Our morning scripture routine has been a good anchor. What's one way we could make that time together even more meaningful or consistent?

Day Three · Saturday

Held, Not Performing

Our worth was never meant to come from a perfectly stable family. It was meant to come from being held by God, even when everything around us feels shaky.

Prayer

Ask God for the security to rest in being chosen by him, not in performing well enough to earn stability. Pray for peace over whatever feels shaky right now.

Ephesians 1:4-5

According as he hath chosen us in him before the foundation of the world, that we should be holy and without blame before him in love: having predestinated us unto the adoption of children by Jesus Christ to himself, according to the good pleasure of his will.

Psalm 62:5-6

My soul, wait thou only upon God; for my expectation is from him. He only is my rock and my salvation: he is my defence; I shall not be moved.

Talk it through
  • Where have we been looking for security in things that can't actually hold it?
  • How does knowing we're chosen by God change how we respond when our families feel unstable?

Day Four · Sunday

Setting the Anchor

An anchor lets a boat stop fighting the waves and just rest. This whole weekend has been pointing here. Now it's time to actually stop striving.

Prayer

Ask God to help you carry this rest home with you, not just feel it for a weekend. Pray for the courage to protect it once normal life starts back up.

Matthew 11:28-29

Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest. Take my yoke upon you, and learn of me, for I am meek and lowly in heart, and ye shall find rest unto your souls.

Exodus 20:8-10

Remember the sabbath day, to keep it holy. Six days shalt thou labour, and do all thy work, but the seventh day is the sabbath of the LORD thy God, in it thou shalt not do any work.

Talk it through
  • What's one moment this weekend where we actually felt rested and anchored in him, not just physically but really at peace? What made that possible?
  • What makes it hard to carry that kind of rest into our normal daily life? What's one thing we could change to protect it?
