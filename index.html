A Beach Weekend Devotional

Anchored

Four days for the two of us. To reconnect, get rooted, and finally rest.

"We have this hope as an anchor for the soul, firm and secure." Hebrews 6:19

Ethan & Caroline · Thursday–Sunday

Thu · Day 1 Fri · Day 2 Sat · Day 3 Sun · Day 4

Each day builds on the last, the way a chain runs down to what actually holds. Read it together and slow down wherever you want to. Where a question is marked each of you, both answer it before you start discussing, so the first answer doesn't decide the second.

Day One · Thursday

Anchored in Our Marriage

Genesis 2:24 says a man leaves his father and mother and bonds to his wife. Leaving isn't rejection; it's a real change of address. The two of you are no longer visitors in each other's families, you're building the house guests will visit. That's what "one flesh" means: a new, singular thing where two used to stand separately.

That takes courage, because the families you came from are the only pattern either of you has ever seen up close. Some of it is worth keeping. Some of it isn't yours to keep just because it's familiar. This weekend is a good time to decide, on purpose, instead of drifting into whatever's easiest to repeat.

Prayer

Ask God to root your marriage first in him. Pray that as a couple you would become a steady anchor for each other, rather than drifting toward whatever shaped you growing up.

Genesis 2:24

This is why a man leaves his father and mother and bonds with his wife, and they become one flesh.

Hebrews 6:19

We have this hope as an anchor for the soul, firm and secure. It enters the inner sanctuary behind the curtain.

Talk it through
  • Each of youWhat's one thing from your family growing up you want to carry into ours, and one thing you want to end with us?
  • Which of the things you want to end have you already caught yourself doing?
  • If our grandkids trace this family back to a starting point, what do we want them to find when they get here?

Day Two · Friday

Anchored in the Word

Psalm 1 doesn't say the blessed man reads his Bible. It says he delights in it, the way you'd delight in something you actually wanted. That's a different posture than duty. A tree planted by a stream doesn't work to get water, it's just positioned where the water already is, and the fruit is a byproduct of the placement, not the effort.

Colossians 2:7 calls that being "rooted": past tense, already done, something you keep building on rather than keep re-earning. Ask honestly this weekend whether your time in scripture together has felt more like a stream you're planted by, or a task you're getting through.

Prayer

Ask God for a real hunger for his word this weekend, not obligation. Pray that his truth would feel like solid ground under both of you.

Psalm 1:1-3

How happy is the one who does not walk in the advice of the wicked or stand in the pathway with sinners or sit in the company of mockers! Instead, his delight is in the LORD's instruction, and he meditates on it day and night. He is like a tree planted beside flowing streams that bears its fruit in its season, and its leaf does not wither. Whatever he does prospers.

Colossians 2:7

being rooted and built up in him and established in the faith, just as you were taught, and overflowing with gratitude.

Talk it through
  • Each of youWhen did scripture last tell you something you didn't want to hear? Did you obey it, or negotiate with it?
  • What are you believing about God right now based on how life feels, rather than on what he's actually said?
  • Honestly, what has our morning time in the word been like for you lately? What would make it something you'd miss if we skipped it?

Day Three · Saturday

Anchored in Him

Ephesians 1 puts the choosing before the foundation of the world, before either of you did anything to earn it or lose it. That timing matters. If God chose you before you existed, nothing you've done since can be the real reason you're his.

Psalm 62 calls him a rock and a stronghold, and says "I will not be shaken," not because life stopped being shaky but because what you're standing on isn't life. Where you've been quietly trying to earn a security that was never for sale is usually where the shaking gets in.

Prayer

Ask God for the security to rest in being chosen by him, not in performing well enough to earn stability. Pray for peace over whatever feels shaky right now.

Ephesians 1:4-5

For he chose us in him, before the foundation of the world, to be holy and blameless in love before him. He predestined us to be adopted as sons through Jesus Christ for himself, according to the good pleasure of his will.

Psalm 62:5-6

Rest in God alone, my soul, for my hope comes from him. He alone is my rock and my salvation, my stronghold; I will not be shaken.

Talk it through
  • Each of youWhen life gets shaky, what's the first thing you reach for to feel okay again?
  • Is there anywhere you still feel like you have to earn your place: at work, with your family, or with me?
  • Is there anything you need to hear me apologize for?

Day Four · Sunday

Anchored in Rest

Jesus doesn't say come to me and I'll help you work more efficiently. He says come, and I will give you rest: his to give, not yours to produce. The yoke he offers replaces the one you're already wearing, it doesn't just get lighter.

The Sabbath in Exodus 20 isn't a reward for finishing your work. It comes on a fixed day whether the work is done or not, which is the point: rest that has to wait for everything to be finished never actually arrives. This weekend's rest is only real if you build a way to protect a shape of it once you're home.

Prayer

Ask God to help you carry this rest home with you, not just feel it for a weekend. Pray for the courage to protect it once normal life starts back up.

Matthew 11:28-29

Come to me, all of you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest. Take my yoke upon you and learn from me, because I am lowly and humble in heart, and you will find rest for your souls.

Exodus 20:8-10

Remember the Sabbath day, to keep it holy: You are to labor six days and do all your work, but the seventh day is a Sabbath to the LORD your God. You must not do any work.

Talk it through
  • Each of youWhat's one moment this weekend you actually felt at rest? What made it possible?
  • Name the specific thing, not "busyness" but the actual thing, most likely to pull us back into striving by Wednesday.
  • What's one thing we will protect when we get home, and who says something out loud when we start slipping?
